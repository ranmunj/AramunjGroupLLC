Before we begin

A few useful answers.

Can you help a small business with just one problem?

Yes. We can scope a focused project such as cleaning up a database, upgrading an application, building a website, or improving a report. A larger transformation is not a prerequisite.

Can you work with our existing software?

We start by assessing what you already use. The recommendation may be an upgrade, an integration, a targeted rebuild, or a migration, depending on the system's condition and your goals.

Do you deliver projects outside the United States?

Yes. We work with organizations internationally through remote collaboration. Time-zone coverage, regional requirements, delivery milestones, and support arrangements are agreed for each engagement.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

We review your needs and contact you to discuss your current systems, goals, and timeline. We then agree next steps and, where appropriate, prepare a scope and proposal.
