Who We Are

Technology delivery for organizations ready to build, modernize, and scale.

Aramunj Group LLC is a full-service IT consulting and enterprise technology solutions company established in Texas in 2021.

We help organizations translate strategy into reliable software, cloud platforms, data ecosystems, secure integrations, and measurable operational outcomes. Our work spans technology roadmaps, product planning, custom application development, quality assurance, DevOps, cloud modernization, cybersecurity readiness, analytics, and managed support.

We are built for organizations that need more than isolated development work. Aramunj brings architecture, implementation, governance, and executive-level advisory together so systems are designed correctly, delivered responsibly, and supported beyond launch.

We partner with enterprises, public-sector agencies, financial institutions, municipalities, and growing businesses that want trusted, scalable, secure, and intelligent technology foundations for long-term growth.

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Ranga Munjanja

Ranga Munjanja is the Founder and CEO of Aramunj Group LLC.

With over 10 years of enterprise technology experience, Ranga specializes in enterprise architecture, software delivery, cloud modernization, data governance, analytics, AI readiness, and digital transformation.

Throughout his career he has contributed to enterprise technology initiatives supporting major financial institutions including Bank of America, Citi, and Credit One Bank.

His work has included enterprise cloud architecture, database modernization, application integration, data engineering, governance frameworks, business intelligence, centralized data platforms, and secure enterprise delivery.

Today he leads Aramunj Group LLC while also spearheading international digital transformation initiatives including Zimbabwe Global Talent Network and the Zimbabwe Data Platform Initiative.
