Who We Are

Trusted data foundations for organizations ready to modernize.

Aramunj Group LLC is a technology consulting and enterprise data solutions company established in Texas in 2021.

We specialize in helping organizations centralize fragmented data, modernize legacy systems, implement secure cloud architectures, strengthen governance, and unlock data-driven decision making.

Our expertise spans enterprise architecture, cloud modernization, analytics, AI readiness, business intelligence, database modernization, and public-sector digital transformation.

We partner with organizations looking to build trusted, scalable, and intelligent data ecosystems that support long-term growth.

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Ranga Munjanja

Ranga Munjanja is the Founder and CEO of Aramunj Group LLC.

With over 10 years of enterprise technology experience, Ranga specializes in enterprise data architecture, cloud modernization, data governance, analytics, AI readiness, and digital transformation.

Throughout his career he has contributed to enterprise technology initiatives supporting major financial institutions including Bank of America, Citi, and Credit One Bank.

His work has included enterprise cloud architecture, database modernization, data engineering, governance frameworks, business intelligence, centralized data platforms, and secure enterprise integrations.

Today he leads Aramunj Group LLC while also spearheading international digital transformation initiatives including Zimbabwe Global Talent Network and the Zimbabwe Data Platform Initiative.
